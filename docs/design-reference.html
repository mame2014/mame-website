Small by design.

MAME Capital GmbH was founded in Zürich in 2014 and works as a small team of associates, led by its founder. A network built one relationship at a time across Switzerland, Europe and the Gulf.

No investment advice, no product structuring, no transaction execution. That keeps us independent of every party at the table — which is why the table opens.
